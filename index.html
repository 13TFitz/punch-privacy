Punch 2.0 Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Punch 2.0 (“Punch”) is a game by Tom Fitzgerald for iPhone, iPad, Mac (as a “Designed for iPad” app), and Messages. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Punch does not collect, upload, sell, or share your personal information.
  • Punch has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • Punch does not connect to the internet or to any server run by us.
  • Punch does not use your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or location.
  • The few things Punch remembers are kept only on your device.
  • Anything you send to other people is sent only when you choose to send it.

What stays on your device

Punch stores a small amount of information locally, on your device only:

  • History. When you finish sharing a punch from the main app, Punch saves a row in its History list: which hit (or hits, for a Sequence), the line you wrote (up to 80 characters), and the date and time. The list keeps at most your 200 most recent entries. You can delete one entry or clear the whole list at any time from the History screen.
  • Settings. Your Sound and Haptics on/off choices.
  • Temporary movie and picture files. When you tap Send, Punch makes a short movie (or a still picture if a movie can’t be made) of your hit on your device and puts it in the app’s temporary folder so it can be shared. Your device’s system may clear these files at any time.
  • Apple Watch. If you use Punch on Apple Watch, the watch keeps a simple count of how many hits you’ve played on that watch.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it from your device.

Sharing is always your choice

Punch only sends something when you tap Send:

  • In the main app, Send opens the standard iOS share sheet. You pick where the movie or picture goes (for example Messages, Mail, or Save Video), or you can cancel.
  • In the Messages extension, Send places the movie (or a still picture) of the hit, which can include the line you wrote, into the conversation you have open.

Once you share something, it is handled by the app or service you chose (such as Apple’s Messages) and by the people you sent it to, under their own privacy policies. Punch does not receive a copy, and clearing Punch’s History does not delete messages you already sent.

What we don’t do

  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools.
  • No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • No data sold or shared with third parties.

Children and age rating

Punch is intended for adults. It is rated 17+ on the App Store because it includes cartoon violence (punches and kicks that “crack” the screen) and a “Middle Finger” hit. Punch is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of information changes, we will update this policy and its effective date above.
